How this design maps to NIST guidance. This is a design mapping, not an assessment or authorization to operate.

Levels and FIPS 199 impact

REDHigh impact
Named people only. Not hierarchical, so no base level, role or rank reaches it.
AMBERModerate impact
Users with base level AMBER. Temporary access needs one approver.
GREENLow impact
All authorized users. Every access is still decided and logged.

Access decision flow, SP 800-162

1. PAP
Policy administration
Operation access screen, changes need dual authorization.
2. PIP
Policy information
Roster, read-in records, session assurance and device state.
3. PDP
Policy decision
Evaluates POL-RED-NAMED and returns Permit or Deny.
4. PEP
Policy enforcement
Registry, search and viewer. The HSM releases a key only after Permit.
